## ✨ Features

- 🥺 **Escalating "Please Think Again" Progression**:
  - Starts with the cute proposal: *"Do you love me? 🥺👉👈"*
  - Every time "No" is clicked, it cycles through heartfelt and hilarious pleas:
    - *"Wait... are you sure? Please think again!"*
    - *"Please think again! Pretty please with strawberries on top? 🍓🥺"*
    - *"Look how sad you're making me! Please think again! 😭💔"*
    - *"I'm literally crying real tears right now... Think again please! 🌊😭"*
    - *"Okay, now you're just being a mischievous little menace! 😤💖"*
  - The **"Yes" button swells larger and larger** with each "No", glowing with a romantic pulse!
  - **Dodge Mode**: Once they click "No" enough times, the "No" button begins playfully running away from their cursor or tap!

- 🎉 **Grand Celebration Finale**:
  - Heart-shaped multi-cannon confetti bursts across the screen.
  - Sweet kissing bears celebration animation (`celebration.gif`).
  - Triumphant romantic harp fanfare audio effect.

- 🎟️ **Interactive "Dream Date Pass" Voucher**:
  - Customize the date plan (Candlelight dinner, cozy cafe, late-night movie, sunset walk, arcade games).
  - Pick food (Pizza & pasta, sushi, tacos, burgers, chocolate & ice cream).
  - Pick date time (This weekend, Valentine's Day, etc.).
  - **1-Click WhatsApp Share** or **Copy Pass to Clipboard**.

- 💌 **Secret Wax-Sealed Love Letter Modal**:
  - An animated interactive envelope with a golden wax seal.
  - Opens into a romantic handwritten parchment note from the sender to the recipient.

- 🎵 **Built-in Lo-Fi Music Box & Sound FX**:
  - Original soothing romantic chord progression synthesized via the Web Audio API (100% offline, zero broken links, works instantly).
  - Squeaks, whooshes, bubble pops, and celebration chimes.

- ✨ **Interactive Sparkle Heart Background**:
  - Ambient floating hearts and stickers.
  - Tap or click anywhere on the background to spawn floating love hearts!
  - Click floating ambient hearts to pop them with audio feedback!

- ⚙️ **Personalization & Direct Link Sharing**:
  - Set recipient's name and sender's name.
  - Generates a custom URL with query params (e.g. `http://localhost:5173/?to=Ragavi&from=Saravanan`) that instantly customizes the greetings and letters!
